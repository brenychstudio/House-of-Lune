# ADR 0007: Site-native content and spatial boundary

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-05

## Decision

Keep canonical public content typed and versioned with the site. Treat spatial presentation as progressive enhancement behind a manifest/capability boundary, never as the sole carrier of product meaning or controls.

## Consequences

The foundation remains accessible, indexable when authorized, and complete on constrained devices. A future spatial runtime can fail or be withheld without removing product truth.
